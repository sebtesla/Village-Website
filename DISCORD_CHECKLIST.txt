
                  DISCORD OAUTH SETUP CHECKLIST                       ║
                     therustvillage.netlify.app                       ║




 Navigate to: OAuth2 → General
 Add Redirect: https://therustvillage.netlify.app/api/auth/callback/discord
 Click "Save Changes"






Add these 4 variables:


 1. NEXT_PUBLIC_DISCORD_CLIENT_ID                                  │
    <your-discord-client-id>                                       │

 2. DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET                                           │
    <your-discord-client-secret>                                   │

 3. NEXTAUTH_SECRET                                                 │
    <your-nextauth-secret>                                         │

 4. NEXTAUTH_URL                                                    │
    https://therustvillage.netlify.app                              │


 Click "Add a variable" for each one
 Netlify will automatically redeploy





  Wait 2-3 minutes for Netlify to redeploy








Total Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Easy
