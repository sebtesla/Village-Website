# 🎨 How to Add Your Favicon

## ✅ Quick Setup (2 Minutes)

Your logo is ready! Just follow these steps:

### Step 1: Save Your Logo

1. **Right-click on the logo image** you uploaded (the shield with windmill)
2. **Save it** to your computer as `favicon.png`

### Step 2: Upload to Project

**Option A: Via Same IDE**
1. In Same, navigate to `my-merch-store/public/`
2. Click "Upload file"
3. Upload your `favicon.png`

**Option B: Replace Existing**
1. Find `my-merch-store/public/village-logo.png`
2. Replace it with your new logo
3. Rename to `favicon.png`

### Step 3: Create Multiple Sizes (Optional)

For best compatibility, create these sizes from your logo:
- `public/favicon.ico` - 32x32 or 48x48
- `public/icon.png` - 512x512
- `public/apple-icon.png` - 180x180

**Quick Tool:** Use https://realfavicongenerator.net/
1. Upload your logo
2. Download the generated files
3. Place them in `my-merch-store/public/`
