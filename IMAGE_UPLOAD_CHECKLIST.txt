STEP 1: PREPARE YOUR PHOTOS


For each product, take 3-5 photos:

 Photo 1: Front view (main image)
 Photo 2: Back view
 Photo 3: Detail shot (logo, texture, etc.)
 Photo 4: Side view or lifestyle shot
 Photo 5: Product being worn/used

Photo Requirements:
 At least 800x800 pixels
 Good lighting (natural light is best)
 Clean background (white or light gray)
 Product centered and in focus
 File size under 500KB


STEP 2: UPLOAD TO IMGUR


 1. Go to: https://imgur.com/upload
 2. Click "New post" or drag images onto page
 3. Upload all photos for one product
 4. Wait for uploads to complete

For EACH uploaded image:
 5. Right-click the image
 6. Select "Copy image address"
 7. Paste URL into a text file

You should have URLs like:
  https://i.imgur.com/ABC123.jpg
  https://i.imgur.com/DEF456.jpg
  https://i.imgur.com/GHI789.jpg

 URLs must start with: https://i.imgur.com/
 URLs must end with: .jpg or .png
 Do NOT use: https://imgur.com/ABC123 (wrong!)

STEP 4: VERIFY


 Check homepage: Product appears with main image
 Check shop page: Product visible in correct category
 Click product: All images show in gallery
 Test on mobile: Images load and are responsive


TROUBLESHOOTING


Image not showing?
  □ Check URL starts with https://i.imgur.com/
  □ Check URL ends with .jpg or .png
  □ Open URL in browser - should show just the image
  □ Try clearing cache (Ctrl+Shift+R)

Can't access admin panel?
  □ Password is: admin123
  □ URL is: /admin/products

Product not in shop?
  □ Make sure "In Stock" is checked
  □ Wait 1 minute and refresh page
